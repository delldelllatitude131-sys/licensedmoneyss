Understanding What a Licensed Money Lender in Clementi Actually Means

Under Singapore law, a licensed money lender is a financial institution that has received formal approval from the Ministry of Law (MinLaw) to provide loans under strict regulatory oversight.

The significance of choosing a regulated lender cannot be overstated. Licensed lenders must adhere to the Moneylenders Act and Rules, ensuring fair treatment and transparent loan conditions.
